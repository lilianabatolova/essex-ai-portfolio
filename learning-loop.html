EMERGING LEARNING

How My Thinking Is Developing

From AI Delivery to AI Understanding

My professional experience initially gave me a strong understanding of AI delivery, operating models, governance, and organisational adoption. The MSc is increasingly adding the technical foundations needed to understand what happens inside the systems being delivered.

From Model Performance to Decision Context

My understanding of AI quality is becoming broader than technical performance alone. Model selection and evaluation must also consider data quality, explainability, consequences of error, human oversight, and the decision the system is intended to support.

From Governance as Control to Governance by Design

Responsible AI is increasingly becoming part of how I think about the complete AI lifecycle rather than a compliance activity applied after technical development has finished.

From Participation to Critical Position

Collaborative discussions and tutor feedback are reinforcing the importance of making my own analytical position explicit, challenging assumptions, and supporting substantive arguments with appropriate academic evidence.
