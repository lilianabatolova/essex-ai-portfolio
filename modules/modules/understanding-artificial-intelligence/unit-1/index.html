UNDERSTANDING ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE

Unit 1: Introduction to Artificial Intelligence

This page documents my learning and reflection from the first unit of the Understanding Artificial Intelligence module.

UNIT OVERVIEW

Learning Focus

Unit 1: Introduction to Artificial Intelligence

Foundations of Artificial Intelligence, historical development, real-world applications, responsible AI, and my initial reflection on the relationship between technical systems and organisational implementation.

KEY LEARNING

Core Themes

Defining Artificial Intelligence

Exploring different interpretations of intelligence and the distinction between systems that imitate intelligent behaviour and systems that perform specific automated tasks.

Historical Development

Understanding the major stages in the development of AI, including periods of rapid progress, reduced investment, and renewed growth.

AI in Practice

Considering how AI is used in organisations and how successful implementation depends on data, people, processes, governance, and appropriate problem selection.

Responsible AI

Recognising that AI systems can influence individuals and organisations, making fairness, transparency, accountability, and human oversight important from the beginning.

REFLECTION

Initial Reflection

My initial understanding of Artificial Intelligence was strongly influenced by its use in enterprise environments. This unit encourages me to examine the broader theoretical foundations behind the systems and delivery processes I encounter professionally.

One important insight is that AI should not be treated only as a technical solution. Its success also depends on the quality of the problem definition, available data, organisational readiness, governance, and the ability to integrate the solution into existing processes.
