PORTFOLIO STRUCTURE

How Module Evidence Is Organised

Each module portfolio may include:

  • Module overview and learning outcomes
  • Learning reflections and development notes
  • Practical exercises, code, and technical artefacts
  • Selected discussion contributions
  • Tutor and peer feedback
  • Professional skills development
  • Responsible AI and ethical considerations
  • Application of learning to enterprise AI practice

Only material appropriate for public sharing is included. Copyrighted university content, confidential company information, and restricted assessment material are not published.
